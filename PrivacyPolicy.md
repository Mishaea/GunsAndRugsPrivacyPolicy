# Privacy Policy for Guns & Rugs: Idle Tycoon

**Effective date: November 8, 2026**

Halke Studio ("we", "us", or "our") develops and provides **Guns & Rugs: Idle Tycoon** (the "Game"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you play the Game or contact us.

For privacy questions and requests, contact **[nahalkestudio@gmail.com](mailto:nahalkestudio@gmail.com)**.

## 1. Information handled by the Game

### Game progress and settings

The Game stores progress and preferences on your device, including in-game currency, unlocked content, upgrades, tutorial progress, play time, and sound settings. You do not need to create an account with Halke Studio to play.

Local storage is separate from analytics: information about your progress and actions may also be sent to the analytics service described below. Storing progress locally does not mean that all information stays on your device.

### Gameplay analytics and diagnostics

We use **AppMetrica**, a Yandex analytics service, to understand how the Game is used and to diagnose problems. The Game sends events concerning sessions, play time, progression, tutorial steps, in-game actions, orders, in-game economy and upgrades, interface interactions, advertising, and save-related errors. References to purchases in these gameplay events concern spending in-game currency and do not mean that we receive your bank card details.

Events can include a randomly generated session identifier, event sequence, elapsed time, and relevant gameplay values. Crash reporting is enabled. The SDK may also process technical information such as device and app identifiers, IP address, device model, operating system and app version, language, session information, and crash or diagnostic reports, depending on the platform and SDK configuration. An IP address may be used to infer an approximate location.

These identifiers and technical records may qualify as personal information even when they do not include your name.

### Advertising information

The Game uses **Appodeal** and advertising partners connected through its mediation service to display interstitial and rewarded video ads. Rewarded ads provide an in-game benefit when the applicable completion conditions are met.

Advertising services may process advertising identifiers, IP address, device and network information, approximate location inferred from an IP address, and information about ad requests, impressions, clicks, video completion, and performance. The information processed depends on the partner, platform, permissions, settings, and applicable privacy choices.

The Game also creates a random identifier, saves it on your device, and supplies it to Appodeal as a user ID. This identifier is not an account name or email address.

Advertising data is used to deliver and measure ads, manage rewards, prevent fraud, and, where permitted, personalize advertising. Partners may process information under their own privacy policies as well as when providing services to us.

### Information you send us

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments or other information you choose to provide. Please do not send passwords, payment card details, or other information that is unnecessary for your request.

## 2. Why information is used

We use information to:

- operate the Game and maintain local progress and settings;
- understand gameplay and improve balance, usability, and performance;
- investigate crashes, errors, and save problems;
- deliver and measure advertising and provide associated rewards;
- detect fraud and protect the Game and its users;
- answer support and privacy requests and meet legal obligations.

Where data protection law requires a legal basis, the basis depends on the purpose: providing requested functionality, legitimate interests in maintaining and improving the Game where permitted, compliance with legal obligations, or consent where required. This policy is a notice, not a substitute for consent. Merely reading it or continuing to play does not constitute consent to processing that requires a separate choice.

## 3. Services and recipients

The main third-party services involved are:

- **AppMetrica / Yandex:** gameplay analytics and crash diagnostics. See the [Yandex Privacy Policy](https://yandex.com/legal/confidential/en/) and [AppMetrica information about data protection](https://appmetrica.yandex.com/docs/en/new-users/data-security).
- **Appodeal:** advertising mediation and related measurement. See the [Appodeal Privacy Policy](https://appodeal.com/privacy-policy/).
- **Advertising networks and demand partners used through Appodeal:** ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and permitted personalization. See [Appodeal's partner privacy policies and privacy choices](https://appodeal.com/partners-privacy-policies/). This directory covers Appodeal's wider partner ecosystem; it does not mean that every listed partner receives information from every player.
- **Google Play and platform services:** when you download or use the Game through Google Play, Google handles information under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect legal rights and safety, or in connection with a transfer of the Game or its business, subject to applicable law.

We do not sell personal information for money. Disclosures for personalized advertising may nevertheless qualify as a "sale", "sharing", or targeted advertising under some privacy laws. Applicable rights are described below.

## 4. Your choices and rights

Your device's privacy settings may let you delete or limit an advertising identifier or control tracking permissions. Available options vary by operating system. Restricting personalized advertising does not necessarily remove ads or stop all technical processing needed for ad delivery and fraud prevention.

Where an advertising consent or privacy dialog is presented, you can make the choices offered there. You can also consult the partner privacy links above or contact us for help exercising your rights. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information; restrict or object to processing; withdraw consent; or opt out of sale, sharing, or targeted advertising. You may also complain to the relevant data protection authority. We will not discriminate against you for exercising applicable privacy rights.

Send requests to **[nahalkestudio@gmail.com](mailto:nahalkestudio@gmail.com)** with the subject "Guns & Rugs Privacy Request". Explain your request and provide only the information reasonably needed to locate the relevant records. We may ask for additional information to verify the request. Because the Game does not require an account, we may be unable to link a name or email address to device-based records without additional identifiers. We will explain any such limitation and, where appropriate, help direct a request to the relevant provider.

The Game's **Reset Progress** option resets local gameplay progress. It does not delete previously transmitted analytics, advertising records, or all device settings and identifiers. You can clear the app's local data using your operating system's controls where available. Platform backup and restoration settings may affect whether local information is restored later.

## 5. Retention and deletion

Local progress and settings remain on your device until reset, cleared, or removed through available app or operating system controls, subject to backup and restoration behavior.

Analytics and advertising records are held in the relevant providers' systems. Retention depends on the type of data, the purpose of processing, provider retention rules and configured options, and legal requirements. There is no single retention period that applies to all such records. The provider policies linked above explain their practices; you may also contact us about applicable retention and deletion options.

We retain support correspondence while needed to handle the request, resolve related issues, or meet legal obligations. We delete or anonymize identifiable information under our control when it is no longer needed for its purpose or a legal requirement. Information held by providers is subject to their applicable retention and deletion processes. Truly anonymized statistics may be retained longer.

Uninstalling the Game or resetting progress does not itself request deletion of data already received by third-party services. Use the contact process above for such requests. Some information may need to be retained for legal compliance, security, fraud prevention, or legal claims; we will explain applicable exceptions when responding.

## 6. International processing and security

Our service providers operate internationally. Information may be processed outside your country, including in countries with different data protection laws. The provider policies linked above describe their processing arrangements and applicable safeguards. Contact us for information relevant to your request.

We seek to protect information through reasonable technical and organizational measures and by limiting access to what is needed for the purposes described here. No transmission or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Children and teenagers

The Game is intended for users aged **13 and older** and is not directed to children under 13. A content age rating is separate from the intended audience and from the age at which a person can legally consent to data processing.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child under 13 has provided personal information through the Game, contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate steps, including requesting deletion from the relevant service providers.

For users aged 13 and older who are still minors, any processing requiring parental authorization under local law requires that authorization. This policy does not replace a required parental consent process.
